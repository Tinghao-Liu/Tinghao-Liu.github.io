<p align="justify">
I am a final-year PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at University College Cork (UCC), Ireland, working under the supervision of Prof. Guangbo Hao in the Compliant Mechanisms and Robotics (CoMAR) research group. My research focuses on the modeling, nonlinear analysis, and optimization of compliant mechanisms, with particular interest in slender beams and multi-stable structures. 
</p>
<p align="justify">
I hold a Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from UCC and a Bachelor's degree in Mechatronic Engineering from Harbin University of Science and Technology. My expertise includes computational solid mechanics, multi-objective optimization, nonlinear finite element analysis, and additive manufacturing. I have published in peer-reviewed journals such as the International Journal of Mechanical Sciences and Mechanism and Machine Theory, and presented my work at international conferences including WCSMO-15 and euspen.
</p>
<p align="justify">
I have received several recognitions for my academic contributions, including the HEIDENHAIN Scholarship, the Compliant Mechanisms Award from ASME, and the Postgraduate Publication of the Year Award from UCC. With three years of teaching assistant experience, I have delivered tutorials on 3D modeling, FEA simulations, and 3D printing, contributing to postgraduate instruction and hands-on skill development.
</p>
<p align="justify">
I am expected to submit my PhD thesis in October 2025 and am currently seeking postdoctoral research opportunities in mechanical metamaterial design, compliant mechanism design and structural optimization. 
</p>
