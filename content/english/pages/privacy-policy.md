# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 14, 2025_

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit this website.

---

#### 1. Responsibility of Contributors

All content provided on this site reflects the author’s personal research, academic work, or projects. Contributors are responsible for ensuring that any materials shared comply with academic integrity and legal standards. No confidential or copyrighted materials should be uploaded without proper authorization.

---

#### 2. Gathering of Personal Information

We do **not** actively collect personal information from visitors. However, if you choose to get in touch via email or contact form, the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and message) may be stored for communication purposes.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties unless required by law.

---

#### 3. Use of Cookies

This website may use cookies to enhance user experience and for basic analytics (e.g., counting page visits). These cookies do **not** collect personal information.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer not to allow them.

---

#### 4. Protection of Personal Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect any personal information you may submit. However, please be aware that no data transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. You submit information at your own risk.

---

#### 5. Third-Party Links

This site may include links to third-party websites such as GitHub, ResearchGate, or academic institutions. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites.

---

#### 6. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. All changes will be posted on this page. If the changes are significant, we may notify you through the website interface.

**Last updated date will always reflect the latest revision.**
