Research

I study how motion, force and stiffness can be shaped by geometry and elastic deformation rather than by conventional joints and springs. Three connected directions frame the work.

Compliant Mechanisms

Mechanisms that move by elastic deformation of slender members instead of sliding or rotating joints, giving monolithic, friction-free and precise motion. The work covers nonlinear large-deformation modeling and the design of flexure guides, bistable and constant-force mechanisms.

Large deformationBistabilityFlexure design

Origami Engineering

Folding as an engineering tool: flat sheets that transform into compact, deployable and reconfigurable three-dimensional structures. The interest lies in how crease patterns and their kinematics turn into practical mechanisms and structures.

Deployable structuresFolding kinematicsReconfigurable

Mechanical Metamaterials

Architected materials whose unusual behavior comes from the geometry of their unit cells rather than their chemistry. Programmable stiffness, multistability and auxetic response make them a natural extension of compliant and folding mechanisms.

Architected latticesMultistabilityProgrammable

Projects

Selected research and collaborative projects in mechanics, robotics, and AI-assisted engineering design.

2026AI Agents for Injection Mold Design

Contributed to selected parts of a collaborative project using AI agents to assist injection mold design, working as a project contributor within the wider team.

Project contributorAI agentsInjection mold design

2023–2024Nonlinear Modeling and Model-Based Design of Compliant Mechanisms

Developed energy-based models using high-order smooth curvature representations and CoSCME to capture large deformations, axial strain, and bending–stretching coupling. Combined these models with bi-level optimization to tailor the switching forces and negative-stiffness responses of bistable mechanisms.

Large-deformation modelingCoSCMEDesign optimizationModeling paper ↗Optimization paper ↗

2022Compact Flexure Guides for Vibration Energy Harvesting

Led a subproject within the EU Horizon 2020 EnABLES programme on compact flexure guidance for an electromagnetic vibration energy harvester. Designed compliant spring structures for cylindrical integration and low-frequency operation, addressing parasitic motion and directional stiffness through finite element analysis and tests of 3D-printed prototypes.

Subproject leadHorizon 2020 EnABLESEnergy harvestingRelated paper ↗

2021Deployable Structures Based on Bistable Compliant Mechanisms

Designed two families of deployable structures using programmable bistable compliant units: serial assemblies for one-dimensional deployment and cylindrical structures for three-dimensional expansion. Geometric tuning controls deformation range and stability, with the designs evaluated through finite element simulations and experiments.

BistabilityDeployable structuresExperimental validationRelated paper ↗

2019Structural Design of a Hydraulic Biped Robot

Investigated the mechanical architecture and hydraulic system of a biped robot, focusing on lightweight, compact structural design. The work included hydraulic calculations and component selection, with attention to system layout, installation, and oil filtration, establishing a design foundation for subsequent motion-control research.

Mechanical designHydraulic systemsBiped robotics
